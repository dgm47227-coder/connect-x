ConnectX Privacy Policy & Data Safety

Effective Date: October 24, 2026

ConnectX (Android Package ID: com.connectx.social) is a social networking and real-time messaging application powered by Google Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account & Profile Information: When you register or sign in with Google or Email/Password via Firebase Authentication, we store your display name, unique @username, optional bio, and avatar in /users, and your account email address in an isolated private document (/users_private/{uid}) readable only by you.
  • Messages & Stories: Direct 1:1 messages (/chats/{chatId}/messages) are accessible strictly to the two conversation participants. Ephemeral stories (/stories) automatically expire after 24 hours.
  • Device Permissions (Microphone & Camera): Used only during active user-initiated voice or video calls and never recorded on external servers.

2. How Data Is Protected

All data is transmitted over HTTPS/TLS and governed by zero-trust Firebase Cloud Firestore Security Rules. Public user discovery never exposes your email address or private settings.

3. Account & Data Deletion

Users can permanently delete their account and associated data at any time inside the app by navigating to Profile → Account Security & Lifecycle → Delete Account. Deleting your account removes your public profile, private settings, username reservation, and active stories from Cloud Firestore.

4. Contact

For privacy inquiries or data deletion requests, contact the developer via the ConnectX in-app safety & support panel.
